# Ladder data from nesseler

Material from nesseler for Site Safety Patrol: the ladder checklist their protocol is based on, and photos of real ladders on their sites.

## Ladder checklist

[kontrollblatt_checkliste_leitern_tritte.pdf](kontrollblatt_checkliste_leitern_tritte.pdf) (German, 3 pages): the form for the regular ladder inspection, based on the German guideline BGI 694 for ladders and steps.

- **Page 1:** ladder data (inventory number, type, material, number of rungs, length, manufacturer, inspector, next inspection: see inspection sticker)
- **Pages 2–3:** what to check, up to 5 inspections per sheet:
  1. Rails (*Holme*): bent, damaged/cracked, sharp edges, worn
  2. Rungs/steps/platform: bent, damaged, sharp edges, joint to the rail, worn
  3. Spreader (*Spreizsicherung*): complete and fixed, works, damaged
  4. Fittings: damaged/rusty, complete, works, worn, greased
  5. Feet/rollers: complete, worn/damaged, works
  6. Accessories (rail extension, wider feet, wall support): complete and fixed
  7. Marking: instructions/pictograms present
  8. Result: **OK to use** · **repair needed** · **take out of use now**, remarks, next inspection (month/year), date

Your digital protocol should fill in this structure, with a photo as evidence for each point.
